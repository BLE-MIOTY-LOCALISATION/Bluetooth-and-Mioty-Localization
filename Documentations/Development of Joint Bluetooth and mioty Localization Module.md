Development of Joint Bluetooth and mioty Localization Module

The LIKE is developing new methods for low-cost IoT localization. One promising approach combines
Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and mioty within the 2.4 GHz band. This method leverages BLE's low
energy consumption alongside mioty’s long-range capabilities, all on the same hardware. BLE signals
are transmitted at short intervals for precise tracking, while mioty signals are sent less frequently,
enabling device localization even when no BLE receiver is nearby.

As part of a research project, suitable hardware and corresponding firmware for a combined
Bluetooth and mioty system shall be developed, implemented, and tested. The system shall be
designed for ultra-low power consumption, enabling operation with a CR2032 battery.

The tasks are:

-  Review of the existing work on joint Bluetooth and mioty hardware
-  Development of a new system concept
-  Development of the required hardware
-  Development of the required firmware
-  Assembly of the hardware
-

Test of the complete system

All findings will be documented in a written report. Additionally, the source code must be thoroughly
commented for clarity. The student must also adhere to the guidelines of the German Research
Foundation (DFG) for Good Scientific Practice.
